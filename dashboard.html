💡 Key Business Insights & Actions

🎓 Master’s Degree Customers Spend More

Master’s holders spend $334.25 vs $327.88 for Associate degree holders.

→ Target premium offers to advanced-degree professionals.

🐾 Pet Owners = Higher Value

Pet owners spend 1.5% more and engage more recently (528 vs 566 days).

→ Launch pet‑focused promotions & partnerships.

📅 46–60 Age Group is Most Valuable

Age 46–60: $336.67 avg spend vs $327.36 for 60+.

→ Focus upsell campaigns on middle‑aged customers.

🏛️ High Variation in AZ, OH, CA

Spending varies most in Arizona, Ohio, and California.

→ Use personalized campaigns rather than one‑size‑fits‑all regional strategies.

📉 Engagement Does NOT Predict Spend

Correlation = 0.006, p‑value = 0.53 → no significant linear relationship.

→ Engagement metrics are not reliable spending predictors.

📊 Statistical Tests

Gender: p=0.73, Education: p=0.92, State: p=0.35 — no significant differences in avg spend.

→ Demographics are weak predictors of spend; focus on behavioral segmentation.
